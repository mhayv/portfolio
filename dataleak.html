Work About Me

Visualising personal data through an interactive ocean of digital fish.

Role

UX & Experience Design

Duration

8 Weeks

Tools

Figma, Spline

Outcome

Campaign, Mobile Game

Team

Loveday Tan, Danett Pepito, Chance Tavita-Jackson, Beilei Yang

Context

People regularly input personal information online without fully considering its permanence or where it travels after pressing 'submit'. As digital platforms become more integrated into everyday life, the movement and storage of personal data often remains invisible.

Through Data Leak, we aimed to increase awareness of online behaviour and help users understand how their data exists within digital systems through an interactive experience and data visualisation.

Challenge

Propose a design-led response that investigates a specific problem within the digital legacy space. Through research, identify a clear context and audience, and explore how design can make these systems more humane, accessible, ethical, or culturally responsive.

Approach

Visualising data as fish swimming through an ocean of unseen digital systems. We explored how personal data could be translated into visual forms, allowing users to create unique fish that get uploaded into a shared tank. This helps to communicate the idea of data permanence and how information continues to exist once it enters digital spaces.

Outcome

An interactive installation that allowed users to generate digital fish based on their answers in a survey. These fish are added to a shared virtual tank, creating a growing ocean that represented the visibility of data within digital systems.

Research & Insights

We were first drawn to how there are now numbers of apps emerging using AI to forever-ify people. THought it was uncomfortable.

From online research as well as interviews, we found theres a disconnect with how people are responding to ai

Inspiration

I did a case study on Project Liberty Experience.

Also were inspired by things where you draw a fish, and upload it into a digtal tank

Creative Idea

a huge ocean tank where the digital fish represent each persons data. permanence of data, how it moves in often hidden digital systems.

Used fish and ocean as a metaphor, to make the topic more approachable, and easily understandable for the audience.

User Journey and Touchpoints

I developed the user journey of finding, engaging with the campaign

I then planned out how the installation would look like, we decided to place this in one of the Silos in Silo Park, Auckland to make it really look like a tank, as the scene surrounds the audience.

Prototypes and User Testing

we wanted the people to upload their fish, so they would do this by answering a survey, forming their unique fish, and then uploading it to the tank projection.

With Danett, our other Interactive team member, we worked out the flow of the screens and etc.. We integrated an MBTI like thing with the fish outcome, so participants see how they are ig.

We did user testing and found that the context wasnt being explained enough so I went back to update the copy and UI of the screens.

Advanced Prototyping

tested the survey to projection interaction

I created a 3D model using spline to visualise what the installation would look like, and where everything would be placed, to properly understand the user journey

Final outcome

Final outcome is an interactive data visualisation installation.

Reflection

I think the team was so lovely, everyone was very into the project, great environment to share ideas. I also learnt with 3d so also fun.
