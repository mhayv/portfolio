Encouraging people to engage with creative journaling through a playful and reflective public installation.

Role

Solo Academic Project

Duration

8 Weeks

Platform

Adobe InDesign

Outcome

Interactive Experience

Context

Creative journaling has been shown to support reflection, emotional processing, and self-expression. However, many people perceive it as intimidating or requiring artistic skill. This project explores how a public installation could encourage people to experiment with journaling in a more approachable way.

Challenge

Many people are interested in creative hobbies but feel overwhelmed by perfectionism and uncertainty.

Approach

Use research, prototyping, and visual storytelling to create an inviting first experience.

Outcome

A public interactive experience that encourages experimentation and reflection.
